# Excel-Sales-Analytics
A dynamic Sales and Financial dashboard built using Advanced Excel, tracking customer performance, market trends, sales vs targets, and Profit &amp; Loss to enable clear KPI monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
## Business Problem  

Sales and financial data can be difficult to monitor when key performance metrics are not presented in a clear and structured way. This can make it challenging to:
- Track sales performance against targets.
- Identify customer and market performance trends.
- Monitor key financial metrics and profitability.
- Quickly identify performance gaps and areas requiring attention.

This project addresses these challenges by organizing and transforming sales and financial data into an interactive Excel dashboard for clearer KPI tracking and performance analysis. 
___
## Sales Report :

**Project objective:**

1. Develop a Customer Performance Report

2. Conduct a comprehensive comparison between Market Performance against Sales Targets

**Purpose of sales analytics:** Enable businesses to effectively monitor, analyze, and improve sales performance across customers and markets.

**Importance of analyzing sales data:** Identify sales trends, uncover performance patterns, and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to support informed decision-making.

**Role of reports:** Support optimized discount strategies, assist in customer negotiations, and identify high-potential markets for business expansion.
___
## Finance Report :
**Project objective:**

1. Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by Fiscal Year & Months

2. Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by Markets

**Purpose of sales analytics:** Evaluate financial performance, support strategic decision-making, and enhance communication with stakeholders.

**Importance of analyzing Finance data:** Enable benchmarking against historical performance and industry standards while serving as a foundation for budgeting and forecasting activities.

**Role of reports:** Align financial planning with strategic objectives and strengthen confidence in the organization’s financial outlook.

___
## Tools & Skills :

- Proficiency in ETL methodology (Extract, Transform, Load).

- Ability to derive fiscal months and quarters.
- Establishing data model relationships with Power Pivot.
- Proficiency in incorporating supplementary data into an existing data model.
- Utilizing DAX to create calculated columns and measures

## Soft Skills:

 - Strong understanding of Sales & Finance reporting structures.
 - Designing user-centric reports with business usability in mind.
 - Optimization of report performance through fine-tuning techniques.
 - Developing a systematic approach to devising a report building plan.
